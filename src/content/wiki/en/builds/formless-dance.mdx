Formless Dance is the evasion melee-caster hybrid — a build that plays like a glass ninja: close range, high risk, and a dodge stat that does the real tanking.

## Why it works
> *Patch check 2026-08-27 — reviewed against 1.0.30: nothing in 1.0.26–1.0.30 touches this page's weapons, artifacts or mechanics. Recipe unchanged.*
It stacks **Shadow + Spring Song** into a single threat profile: Shadow powers the damage side, Spring Song the mobility, and **Bloodstone Earrings** convert the aggression back into sustain so you don't bleed out between dodges.

## Stat targets

- **Crit rate:** ~60% / **Crit damage:** ~100% — the same split the crit builds use, but reached through the hybrid's dual stacking.
- **Evasion:** stack it, but know the cap (below).

## ⚠️ Evasion cap — read this before investing

**Displayed evasion past ~100 keeps observing diminishing returns; the observed dodge cap sits near 76%.** Don't chase evasion numbers past that point — put the leftover points into crit or HP once you're near the cap. This is the single most common mistake on the route.

## Leveling order

1. **Evasion to the ~76% observed cap** — your survivability floor.
2. Crit rate → ~60%, then crit damage → ~100%.
3. Bloodstone Earrings sustain once you're consistently in melee.

## How to play

Melee-caster rhythm: dive in to stack Shadow, use Spring Song to reposition, and let the dodge stat absorb the errors. The build punishes standing still — keep moving between swings.
