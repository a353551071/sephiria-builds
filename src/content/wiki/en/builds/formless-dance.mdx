Formless Dance is the premier evasion melee-caster hybrid in Sephiria — a build designed to play like a lethal shadow dancer: ultra-close range, high attack speed, continuous repositioning, and a soft-capped dodge stat that serves as your primary defensive barrier.

## Why it works
> *Patch check 2026-09-08 — reviewed against 1.0.30: nothing in 1.0.26–1.0.30 touches this page's weapons, artifacts or mechanics. Recipe unchanged.*

The build interlocks **Shadow + Spring Song** into a self-feeding combat rhythm:
- **Shadow Synergy:** Infuses physical thrusts with dark phantom echos, triggering secondary damage instances on every strike.
- **Spring Song Mobility:** Drastically reduces dodge roll recovery lag and boosts movement speed, letting you glide through projectile storms.
- **Bloodstone Earrings:** Converts relentless aggression into sustain, leeching 2% max HP on hit to instantly top off chip damage taken from non-evaded blows.
- **Duelist Miracle:** Empowers consecutive melee hits with ramping critical strike probability.

## Stat Breakpoints & The Sustain Loop

### ⚠️ Evasion Cap Math: Why You Stop at ~76%
The mathematical reality of Sephiria's combat engine:

```text
Actual Dodge Chance = min(0.76, 1 - 1 / (1 + 0.03 × Displayed Evasion))
```

- Displayed evasion up to 80 scales almost linearly.
- Between 80 and 100, heavy diminishing returns begin.
- Above 100 displayed evasion, the engine clamps actual dodge probability near **76%**.
- **Crucial Rule:** Chasing evasion past ~100 is dead stat weight. Reallocate all extra attribute points into **Crit Rate (~60%)**, **Crit Damage (~100%)**, and **Max HP** to ensure the remaining ~24% un-evaded attacks never one-shot you.

### The Bloodstone Sustain Loop Formula
When an attack connects through the 24% vulnerability window:
1. **Flurry Cadence:** Formless Dance executes a 4-hit thrust flurry in 1.2 seconds.
2. **Health Restoration:** Bloodstone Earrings recovers 2% max HP per registered hit:
   ```text
   HP Restored per Combo = 4 hits × 2% = 8% Max HP
   ```
3. As long as incoming damage per non-evaded hit remains below 40% of your max health bar, you will fully regenerate to 100% HP during the very next attack combo.

### Leveling Order
1. **Floors 1–10:** Staff → Throwing Spear. Prioritize initial Evasion talents until reaching ~50% dodge chance.
2. **Floors 11–20:** Complete Formless Dance at the Anvil. Equip Bloodstone Earrings immediately — do not proceed past Floor 15 without your sustain artifact.
3. **Floors 21+:** Cap Evasion at the ~76% plateau, then direct all subsequent rolls into Crit Rate (60%) and Crit Damage (100%).

## How to play & Boss Execution

- **Melee-Caster Weave Rhythm:** Dive directly into point-blank range to trigger Shadow strike procs, activate Spring Song dashes to reposition out of ground hazards, and let your 76% evasion absorb regular boss swipes.
- **Never Stand Still:** Formless Dance penalizes stationary play. Your dodge engine requires continuous motion inputs to maintain optimal hitbox displacement.
- **Emergency Repositioning:** When a boss prepares an unblockable ground slam or fullscreen telegraph, use Throwing Spear remnants or Spring Song dashes to disengage immediately.
