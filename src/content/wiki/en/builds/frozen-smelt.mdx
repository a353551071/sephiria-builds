Frozen Smelt is the freeze-control route: it stops the screen from playing while your damage catches up. One of the few builds that keeps late-game pressure while enemies are effectively locked down.

> *Patch check 2026-09-10 — game still on 1.0.30 (no release since 08-20). 1.0.26 changed the base **Dagger** (Dash Attack can now chain into Special Attack: Parry/Fury) — a buff to the family's melee layer that does **not** alter the Sleeping Frost → Frozen Smelt freeze core, but re-verify your dash-cancel timing after the change (details on the [Dagger weapon page](/weapons/dagger)). 1.0.30's Frozen Bow arrow-pierce change is a different weapon and does not touch this line.*

## Why It Works

**Glacier + Precision** over a frozen screen. Freeze does double duty — it's survivability (frozen enemies don't attack) and damage (Glacier hits harder on frozen targets). The three ice artifacts feed the same loop: **Ice Snow + Ice Seagull + Frozen Egg** stack freeze uptime and cold damage.

The weapon line makes one deliberate trade at the Sleeping Frost step: **your Weapon ATK loses 20% of its base damage, but all of it converts to scale off Cold DMG**. That trade is what turns every cold stat you pick up into global scaling — the dagger stops being a physical weapon and becomes a freeze applicator that happens to do damage. Frozen Smelt then completes the engine: **every Special ATK hit applies Frostbite twice**, doubling your stack cadence on the exact targets you're about to freeze solid.

## Stat Breakpoints & Freeze Loop Math

### The Cold Conversion Trade

At the Sleeping Frost step the compendium lists the exchange explicitly: **-20% Weapon ATK DMG, damage now scales off Cold DMG**. The practical reading:

1. **The -20% is a one-time toll, not a scaling penalty** — it shaves the baseline, then every Cold DMG point you acquire raises the whole converted packet.
2. **Frostbite cadence is the real control stat.** Frozen Smelt's capstone applies **two Frostbite applications per Special ATK hit**. Since freeze uptime is what keeps Glacier's bonus live (and keeps you alive), the Special ATK is your rotation's metronome — not a damage button you spam, but the double-stack you place deliberately.
3. **Wisdom ~5 is the MP floor**, not a target — the build runs on control, so over-stacking casting stats dilutes the freeze loop.

### The Re-Freeze Window

The discipline that separates a working run from a stall: **re-freeze before the first freeze expires**. The ice trio's uptime is what bridges the gap between Frostbite applications. If the gap opens, frozen targets thaw, Glacier loses its bonus, and the -20% conversion toll is suddenly being paid without the control that justified it.

### Leveling Order

1. **Wisdom to ~5 first** — the MP floor that keeps the freeze loop sustainable.
2. Freeze uptime artifacts (the ice trio) before raw damage.
3. **Berut's Sickle when it appears** — the single biggest DPS jump on this route.

## How to Play

Hold the screen. Keep Glacier ticking on frozen groups and re-freeze before the first freeze expires — the artifact set's uptime is what separates a working run from a stall. Hunter Miracle keeps the pace while you kite. One family note: the dagger tree's other frost capstone (Drífa) evolves the **Blizzard Hammer relic** into a targeted Blizzard Scythe instead of the weapon itself — a different build's engine, but a reminder that the frost toolkit is deep if this loop clicks for you.
