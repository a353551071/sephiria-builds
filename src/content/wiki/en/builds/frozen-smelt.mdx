Frozen Smelt is the freeze-control route: it stops the screen from playing while your damage catches up. One of the few builds that keeps late-game pressure while enemies are effectively locked down.

> *Patch check 2026-08-27 — reviewed against 1.0.30. 1.0.26 changed the base **Dagger** (Dash Attack can now chain into Special Attack: Parry/Fury) — a buff to the family's melee layer that does **not** alter the Sleeping Frost → Frozen Smelt freeze core, but re-verify your dash-cancel timing after the change (details on the [Dagger weapon page](/weapons/dagger)). 1.0.30's Frozen Bow arrow-pierce change is a different weapon and does not touch this line.*

## Why it works

**Glacier + Precision** over a frozen screen. Freeze does double duty — it's survivability (frozen enemies don't attack) and damage (Glacier hits harder on frozen targets). The three ice artifacts feed the same loop: **Ice Snow + Ice Seagull + Frozen Egg** stack freeze uptime and cold damage.

## Stat targets

- **Wisdom:** ~5 — just enough for MP; the build runs on control, not raw casting.
- **Late DPS:** ≈10K, rising to ≈17K with Berut's Sickle.

## Leveling order

1. **Wisdom to ~5 first** — the MP floor that keeps the freeze loop sustainable.
2. Freeze uptime artifacts (the ice trio) before raw damage.
3. **Berut's Sickle** when it appears — the single biggest DPS jump on this route.

## How to play

Hold the screen. Keep Glacier ticking on frozen groups and re-freeze before the first freeze expires — the artifact set's uptime is what separates a working run from a stall. Hunter Miracle keeps the pace while you kite.

## Evidence & patch notes

**Hard 60 claimed on 1.0.24.** The DPS figures (≈10K / ≈17K) are community-reported rather than video-verified — treat the ceiling as strong-but-not-gospel. See the [six-build overview](/builds/best-builds) for the full comparison.
