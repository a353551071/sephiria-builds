Biryuu is the katana's final evolution and the undisputed peak crit setup in the 1.0 meta — the benchmark against which every other melee build is judged. Operating as an aggressive, high-risk melee glass cannon, Biryuu exploits a unique engine mechanic: every single swing registers simultaneously as a normal attack and a final hit.

## Why it works
> *Patch check 2026-09-08 — reviewed against 1.0.30: nothing in 1.0.26–1.0.30 touches this page's weapons, artifacts or mechanics. Recipe unchanged.*

Biryuu's attacks count as **both normal attacks and final hits**. That quirk doubles the value of on-hit effects:
- **Firmness:** Stacks bonus weapon damage on normal hit. Because Biryuu swings qualify as normal attacks, each swing applies 2 stacks instead of 1.
- **Precision:** Stacks bonus critical damage on final hits. Because Biryuu swings qualify as final hits, it simultaneously gains 2 stacks of Precision per swing.
- **Compounding Damage Curve:** Instead of relying on flat attack power, Biryuu converts swing count into compounding, exponentially scaling critical strikes.
- **Hunter Miracle:** Bestows stacking attack speed and crit chance buffs during continuous melee uptime, keeping your flurry uninterrupted.

## Stat Breakpoints & Double-Dip Math

### The Double-Dip Multiplication Formula
Because Biryuu possesses both `Normal Attack` and `Final Hit` classification flags simultaneously, each physical swing calculates damage through dual on-hit bonuses:

```text
Effective Hit Value = Base Slash DMG × (1 + Firmness Bonus × 2) × (Crit Multiplier + Precision Bonus × 2)
```

- **Firmness Ramp:** At maximum stacks (10 stacks), normal builds receive +50% ATK. Biryuu reaches this cap in **5 swings** rather than 10.
- **Precision Ramp:** Precision adds +5% Crit DMG per stack up to 10 stacks (+50% Crit DMG). Biryuu hits max cap in **5 swings**, instantly inflating critical strikes from 100% to 150%+ effective damage.

### The 60/100–130 Crit Sweet Spot
- **Crit Rate (~60%):** At 60% crit chance with an attack cadence of 3.2 swings/sec, the mathematical probability of experiencing 3 consecutive non-crits is `(1 - 0.60)^3 = 6.4%`. Damage output remains smoothly predictable with zero DPS droughts. Chasing crit rate past 60% yields severe diminishing returns due to talent point opportunity costs.
- **Crit Damage (~100–130%):** Once the 60% floor is locked, every additional point in Crit Damage directly multiplies total output without wasting budget on surplus rate rolls.

### Leveling Order
1. **Floors 1–10:** Katana → Cheongwangsak. Prioritize Crit Rate talents until reaching ~40%.
2. **Floors 11–20:** Acquire Firmness and Precision at shops or treasure vaults. Upgrade to Biryuu at the Anvil.
3. **Floors 21+:** Push Crit Rate to 60%, stack Crit Damage toward 100–130%, and maintain high Hunter Miracle uptime.

## How to play & Boss Execution

- **Continuous Swing Rhythm:** Biryuu requires relentless aggression. If you disengage for longer than 2.0 seconds, your double-stacked Firmness and Precision buffs expire.
- **I-Frame Navigation:** Katana draw and sheath animations grant **0.18-second invulnerability frames (i-frames)**. Use these micro-windows to cut through boss sweeping attacks without rolling away.
- **Transitioning from Tank to DPS:** If you are unfamiliar with Floor 25+ boss tell-animations, start with `[PRESET:SEPH-1030-BK-TK60]`. Once you can consistently dodge unblockable slams, switch to `[PRESET:SEPH-1030-BK-CRIT60]` for maximal clearing speed.
