Biryuu is the katana's final evolution and the strongest crit setup on the board on 1.0.2x — the build every other melee route is measured against.

## Why it works
> *Patch check 2026-08-27 — reviewed against 1.0.30: nothing in 1.0.26–1.0.30 touches this page's weapons, artifacts or mechanics. Recipe unchanged.*
Biryuu's attacks count as **both normal attacks and final hits**. That quirk doubles the value of on-hit effects: Firmness and Precision each stack twice per swing, so the build converts swing count into a compounding damage curve instead of a flat stat line.

## Stat targets

- **Crit rate:** ~60%
- **Crit damage:** ~100%

The 60/100 split is where the community converged: crits land consistently enough to feel reliable, without starving every other stat to get there.

## Leveling order

1. **Crit rate toward ~60% first** — consistent crits beat burst while you're learning the rhythm.
2. **Crit damage to ~100%** — once the rate floor is in place.
3. **Firmness / Precision** as the on-hit artifact backbone, since each swing doubles them.

## How to play

Keep swings landing. Because Biryuu double-procs on-hit effects, uptime is the damage multiplier — stay in attack rhythm rather than fishing for big single hits. Position to keep the Hunter Miracle buff up between engage windows.

## Tank variant (Blade Katana)

Want the same tree with far more forgiveness? **Blade Katana with a maxed Guardian stat** has cleared **Tier 60** and is the recommended entry point before graduating to the glass-cannon Biryuu. Start here, swap to Biryuu once you can read boss attack patterns.
