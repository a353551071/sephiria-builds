These are the six Sephiria builds with the strongest evidence behind them on patch 1.0.2x. Every recipe lists the full weapon evolution path, Miracle, costume and core artifacts. Where a claim is community-reported rather than video-verified, we say so — treat anything labeled "community claim" as very strong but not gospel.

## Biryuu Katana (crit)

**Path:** Katana → Cheongwangsak → Biryu · **Costume:** Red Fox · **Miracle:** Hunter

Biryuu's attacks count as both normal attacks and final hits, so on-hit effects like Firmness and Precision stack twice per swing. Build around ~60% crit chance and ~100% crit damage. A tank variant — Blade Katana with a maxed Guardian stat — has cleared Tier 60 and is far more forgiving. Full details on the [Katana page](/weapons/katana).

## Creaking Spine (greatsword)

**Path:** Steel Greatsword → Bone Mechanism Sword → Creaking Spine · **Stats:** Will 20 / Patience 20 · **Miracle:** Valet

The community's default "defense before damage ceiling" pick (58 likes on the recipe). Greatsword special-attack builds pair Absolute Ring and Amethyst crystals with companion and mana damage; Rock Elephant is the named damage piece. Lowest legendary-weapon dependence of any route here.

## Frozen Smelt (freeze)

**Path:** Dagger → Sleeping Frost → Frozen Smelt · **Artifacts:** Ice Snow + Ice Seagull + Frozen Egg · **Miracle:** Hunter

Glacier + Precision over a frozen screen. Keep Wisdom around 5 for MP. Community-reported ≈10K late DPS, rising to ≈17K with Berut's Sickle. Hard 60 claimed on 1.0.24.

## Minigun (crossbow)

**Path:** Heavy Crossbow → Unstable Lightning Mark → Minigun · **Core artifacts:** Blue Wrist Guard + Brass Mirror Fragment + Walter's Work Monocle · **Miracle:** Electromancer

Two documented variants: a White Rabbit Hard 35 video preset and a Scholar Lizard tank recipe (Guardian 10 + Magic Engineering 10). Caution: 1.0.23 treated Brass Mirror's below-1 MP interaction as a bug — re-verify after each patch.

## Formless Dance (evasion)

**Path:** Staff → Throwing Spear → Formless Dance · **Miracle:** Duelist · **Sustain:** Bloodstone Earrings

Melee-caster hybrid that stacks Shadow + Spring Song. Target ~60% crit / 100% crit damage; note that displayed evasion past ~100 keeps observing diminishing returns (observed dodge caps near 76%).

## Ilonica (elemental bolts)

**Path:** Staff → Flame Staff → Elemental Fusion Staff Ilonica · **Miracle:** Elementalist · **Costume:** Ghost

Homing three-element bolts. Spread Ember/Glacier/Magitech around 4–6 each. Floor 50 claimed on 1.0.24.

## Copying presets

Several of these builds have shareable preset codes circulating on [r/sephiria](https://www.reddit.com/r/sephiria/) and the [Steam discussions](https://steamcommunity.com/app/2436940/discussions/0/588434434320216892/) — import the code, then adjust artifacts to what your run actually offers. A build you can pilot beats a stronger build you can't.
