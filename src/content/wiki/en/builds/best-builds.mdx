## Per-build breakdowns

Each build below has a full dedicated guide with the complete recipe, leveling order, how-to-play notes and patch caveats.

### Biryuu Katana (crit) — S tier

Biryuu's attacks count as both normal attacks and final hits, so on-hit effects like Firmness and Precision stack twice per swing. Build around ~60% crit chance and ~100% crit damage. A tank variant — Blade Katana with a maxed Guardian stat — has cleared Tier 60 and is far more forgiving.

→ **[Full Biryuu Katana build guide](/builds/biryuu-katana)**

### Creaking Spine (greatsword) — A tier

The community's default "defense before damage ceiling" pick (58 likes on the recipe). Will 20 / Patience 20 with the Valet Miracle; Absolute Ring, Amethyst crystals and Rock Elephant carry the damage. Lowest legendary-weapon dependence of any route here.

→ **[Full Creaking Spine build guide](/builds/creaking-spine)**

### Frozen Smelt (freeze) — A tier

Glacier + Precision over a frozen screen. Ice Snow + Ice Seagull + Frozen Egg artifacts with the Hunter Miracle; keep Wisdom around 5 for MP. Community-reported ≈10K late DPS, rising to ≈17K with Berut's Sickle. Hard 60 claimed on 1.0.24.

→ **[Full Frozen Smelt build guide](/builds/frozen-smelt)**

### Minigun (crossbow) — A tier

Blue Wrist Guard + Brass Mirror Fragment + Walter's Work Monocle with the Electromancer Miracle. Two documented variants: a White Rabbit Hard 35 video preset and a Scholar Lizard tank recipe (Guardian 10 + Magic Engineering 10). ⚠️ 1.0.23 treated Brass Mirror's below-1 MP interaction as a bug — re-verify after each patch.

→ **[Full Minigun build guide](/builds/minigun)**

### Formless Dance (evasion) — B tier

Melee-caster hybrid stacking Shadow + Spring Song with the Duelist Miracle and Bloodstone Earrings sustain. Target ~60% crit / 100% crit damage; displayed evasion past ~100 keeps observing diminishing returns (observed dodge caps near 76%).

→ **[Full Formless Dance build guide](/builds/formless-dance)**

### Ilonica (elemental bolts) — A tier

Homing three-element bolts with the Elementalist Miracle and Ghost costume. Spread Ember/Glacier/Magitech around 4–6 each. Floor 50 claimed on 1.0.24.

→ **[Full Ilonica Bolts build guide](/builds/ilonic-bolts)**

## Copying presets
