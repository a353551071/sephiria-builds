Swing your sword at nothing and watch the sky fall. The meteor build turns **normal attacks** into a screen-wide barrage of burning meteors — the fastest way to turn Sephiria into a screensaver that kills everything.

## Why it works

Three pieces compound into one engine:

1. **Red Snake Eyes** (ember artifact) drops meteors around the screen. Maxed, it lands **4 meteors near the player for 170% fire damage each**, inflicting **burn** on everything hit.
2. **Red Snake Crush** (final form of the sword line, which grows into an axe) makes that engine self-sustaining: Red Snake Eyes deals **+33% damage**, and **every normal attack cuts its cooldown by 2.5 seconds — you don't even need to hit anything**. Swing at the air; the meteors don't care.
3. **Oak Tree Coal** speeds up **how fast burn ticks**. Stack them — the video's run picked up four or five and kept wanting more. Burn damage-over-time is where most of this build's damage actually lives.

Add **Ember Forged** (weapon attacks deal bonus damage equal to 30% of your fire damage) and every swing feeds the fire twice.

## The costume

**Braided Hair Rabbit**: +7% ATK SPD, +12% Move SPD, at the cost of −20% Dash Recovery SPD. The attack speed isn't just comfort — attack speed is meteor frequency while Red Snake Crush is online.

## The current version hits harder than the video

The source video was recorded around the 1.0 launch, when the **Ember combo's Stage 6 effect was bugged and did nothing**. Patch **1.0.26 fixed it**. If you run an ember setup today, you're getting output the video never shows — expect the screen to be even less survivable for the enemies.

## How to play

- **Swing constantly.** With Red Snake Crush, uptime is cooldown reduction is meteor count. Whiffing is fine.
- **Prioritize burn tick speed** (Oak Tree Coal) and flat fire damage when the Anvil or shops offer upgrades. The video's run hit **~1,800 DPS on the training dummy** (versus ~691 for a bolt-based co-op partner).
- Ember artifacts are **unique** — you can't stack a second Red Snake Eyes, so take Oak Tree Coal repeats when they show.
- In **co-op**, this build pairs disgustingly well with bolt/grimoire partners: your meteors blanket the screen while their bolts clean up. (See the [co-op guide](/guides/coop) for party mechanics.)
