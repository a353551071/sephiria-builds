Ilonica is the homing caster line: three-element bolts that track targets so your damage lands even while you're kiting. The most "hands-off" build on this list for raw output.

## Why It Works
> *Patch check 2026-09-11 — game still on 1.0.30 (no release since 08-20); nothing in 1.0.26–1.0.30 touches this page's weapons, artifacts or mechanics. Recipe unchanged.*

The engine is the **Bolt Barrage** conversion. Per the compendium, the final staff form does three things at once:

1. **Your Normal ATK becomes a random Bolt Spell cast** — homing elemental bolts (Ember, Glacier, Magitech) that track targets instead of traveling where you aim.
2. **Each bolt costs 0 MP but puts its own spell on cooldown** — the constraint isn't mana, it's *rotation breadth*: how many distinct bolt types you have available per cycle.
3. **Bolts also benefit from Normal ATK DMG bonuses** — the surprise of the build: although it plays like a caster, weapon-attack damage percentages remain global scaling for the whole barrage.

The trade is equally explicit: **Grimoires cannot be used**. The weapon is your entire spell engine — no backup spellbook. In exchange, uptime isn't gated by your aim or your mana bar; the staff does the aiming and the fuel is free.

## Stat Breakpoints & Barrage Math

### The Triangle: Why 4–6 Each

The community recipe keeps **Ember, Glacier and Magitech within 4–6 and within a point of each other**. The reasoning, from the engine's structure:

1. **Every bolt carries all three elements' payoff** — the barrage casts whatever bolt is off cooldown, so a maxed Ember with stalling Glacier/Magitech means two-thirds of your casts hit like wet paper. Balance keeps every cast meaningful.
2. **The floor is ~4** — below that, bolt damage falls off the cliff that matters; above **6**, the next stat point usually buys more survival (or Ghost costume upgrades) than a fourth point of damage.
3. **Elementalist compounds three-way scaling** — the Miracle rewards breadth, which is exactly what the spread provides.

### Rotation Breadth Is the Real Resource

Because each bolt type carries its own cooldown, the density of your barrage scales with *how many distinct bolt types are available*. A dead cycle (everything on cooldown) is the build's stall state — the 4–6 triangle keeps the common bolt types damaging enough that even partial barrages clear rooms.

### Leveling Order

1. Bring all three elements up to ~4 first — the triangle floor.
2. Then push toward 6 as the run allows, keeping them within a point of each other.
3. Defensive layers (the Ghost costume path) as needed — you're a caster, not a tank.

## How to Play

Kite freely. The homing bolts keep damaging while you reposition, which is the build's whole advantage over aimed casters — use it to stay at the edge of aggro and let Elementalist do the math. Because the barrage fires whenever attack is held and costs nothing, the skill expression is positioning and element management, not button timing.
