Creaking Spine is the community's default **"defense before damage ceiling"** pick — the route most recommended to new players who want to survive long enough to learn boss patterns. (Full greatsword-family evolution tree on the [Greatsword weapon page](/weapons/greatsword).)

## Why it works
> *Patch check 2026-09-10 — game still on 1.0.30 (no release since 08-20); nothing in 1.0.26–1.0.30 touches this page's weapons, artifacts or mechanics. Recipe unchanged.*

Where the crit builds bet everything on killing faster, Creaking Spine bets on **staying alive while the damage comes from a steady, low-maintenance loop**. The Valet Miracle keeps your defensive engine running, and the greatsword's weight lets you trade hits favorably instead of dodging everything.

The engine is the Reassemble special attack. Bone Mechanism Sword converts it into an 8-second window where your Normal ATKs become wide 1-hit swings, and **every consecutive swing during the window deals +15 + 50% of your DEF as bonus damage**. Creaking Spine then widens the swings further and adds a stacking attack-speed ramp — **up to +35% ATK SPD** — the longer you keep the chain unbroken. In other words: the defense stats you stack to survive are the same stats that pay for the damage. Will 20 / Patience 20 is not a compromise, it is the build.

**Absolute Ring** and **Amethyst** crystals pair the loop with companion and mana damage; **Rock Elephant** is the named damage piece that carries the actual kill pressure.

## Stat Breakpoints & Reassemble Math

### The Consecutive-Hit Formula

Per the compendium, each consecutive Normal ATK during Reassemble deals bonus damage of **15 + 50% of your DEF**:

1. **The flat 15** matters early, when DEF is still small — the loop pays out from the very first swing, before any stat investment lands.
2. **The 50%-of-DEF term** is the real curve. Every point of DEF on gear, costumes and Will/Patience stat picks raises the per-swing bonus by half a point — which is why the community recipe maxes both defense stats instead of splitting into offense.
3. **The ATK SPD ramp** (up to +35%) means the window front-loads safety and back-loads damage: early swings are slow and steady, and if you keep the chain alive through the full 8 seconds, the closing swings come out dramatically faster.

### Leveling Order

1. **Will 20 / Patience 20 first** — the whole point is survivability, and the DEF you stack doubles as the Reassemble damage bonus.
2. Companion and mana damage next, since that's what Absolute Ring + Amethyst scale.
3. Rock Elephant as the damage finisher once defense is locked.

### MP Discipline

Reassemble costs 25 MP per activation. The loop is sustainable because Valet and the Amethyst engine keep mana flowing, but the failure mode of this build is opening Reassemble at half mana and losing the window early — treat it as a commitment, not a panic button.

## How to Play

Trade hits deliberately — you out-last most threats. Let companion and mana damage tick while you hold position with the greatsword's reach, and save Reassemble for clear windows rather than panic. Because the consecutive-hit bonus resets when the chain breaks, the skill expression of this build is simple but real: **don't get interrupted**.

## Why Beginners Should Pick This

This is the **lowest legendary-weapon dependence of any route here** — the earlier evolutions (Steel Greatsword → Bone Mechanism Sword) are common enough that the full path is reachable in far fewer runs than the crit or elemental lines. Combined with the forgiving stat profile, it's the route the community hands to new players (58 likes, the most-voted defensive setup on the board).
