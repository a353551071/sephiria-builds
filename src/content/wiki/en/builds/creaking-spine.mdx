Creaking Spine is the community's default **"defense before damage ceiling"** pick — the route most recommended to new players who want to survive long enough to learn boss patterns. (Full greatsword-family evolution tree on the [Greatsword weapon page](/weapons/greatsword).)

## Why it works
> *Patch check 2026-08-27 — reviewed against 1.0.30: nothing in 1.0.26–1.0.30 touches this page's weapons, artifacts or mechanics. Recipe unchanged.*
Where the crit builds bet everything on killing faster, Creaking Spine bets on **staying alive while the damage comes from a steady, low-maintenance loop**. The Valet Miracle keeps your defensive engine running, and the greatsword's weight lets you trade hits favorably instead of dodging everything.

Greatsword special-attack builds pair **Absolute Ring** and **Amethyst crystals** with companion and mana damage; **Rock Elephant** is the named damage piece that carries the actual kill pressure.

## Stat targets

- **Will:** 20
- **Patience:** 20

Both are defense-adjacent stats. Maxing Will 20 / Patience 20 first is the community recipe — it makes the run near-unforgiving while you learn.

## Leveling order

1. **Will 20 / Patience 20 first** — the whole point is survivability.
2. Companion and mana damage next, since that's what Absolute Ring + Amethyst scale.
3. Rock Elephant as the damage finisher once defense is locked.

## How to play

Trade hits deliberately — you out-last most threats. Let companion and mana damage tick while you hold position with the greatsword's reach, and save the special attack for clear windows rather than panic.

## Why beginners should pick this

This is the **lowest legendary-weapon dependence of any route here** — the earlier evolutions (Steel Greatsword → Bone Mechanism Sword) are common enough that the full path is reachable in far fewer runs than the crit or elemental lines.
