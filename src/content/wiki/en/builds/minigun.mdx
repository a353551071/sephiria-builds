Minigun is the machine-gun ranged line — a firehose of crossbow bolts that trades burst for sheer volume. It's the most thoroughly documented build in the 1.0 meta, with a full video-verified Hard 35 clear behind the core recipe.

## Why it works
> *Patch check 2026-09-08 — reviewed against 1.0.30: nothing in 1.0.26–1.0.30 touches this page's weapons, artifacts or mechanics. Recipe unchanged.*

The three core artifacts turn a fast crossbow into a sustained-DPS engine:
- **Blue Wrist Guard:** Feeds rapid attack speed and accelerates reload recovery so your weapon is almost never empty.
- **Brass Mirror Fragment:** Recovers MP on low-energy thresholds, keeping the Special Barrage ability fueled indefinitely.
- **Walter's Work Monocle:** Sharpens every bolt into a precision projectile with heightened crit frequency.
- **Electromancer Miracle:** Amplifies all lightning-flavored payload damage and adds multi-target chain arcing.

## Stat Breakpoints & Barrage Ammo Math

### The 15-Round Volley Formula
1. **Magazine Capacity:** 15 ammo at 4.0/s baseline attack speed yields a full magazine dump in **3.75 seconds**.
2. **Lightning Arrow Procs:** Base 40% from Unstable Lightning Mark + 10% Minigun capstone bonus = **50% Lightning Arrow proc chance per bolt**. Across a 15-bolt Barrage, you average **7 to 8 Lightning Arrows (160% Lightning DMG each)**, plus secondary chain shocks.
3. **MP Cost Cycle:** Barrage consumes **1 MP per shot (15 MP per burst)**. With Brass Mirror Fragment, dropping below 20% MP triggers immediate resource restoration, meaning you can continuously alternate between normal shots and full Barrage channels with zero mana dry-fire.

### Leveling Order
1. **Floors 1–10:** Secure Blue Wrist Guard and evolve to Unstable Lightning Mark. Do not waste gold rerolling for secondary gear until the weapon engine is online.
2. **Floors 11–20:** Complete Minigun at the Anvil and pick up Brass Mirror Fragment.
3. **Floors 21+:** Acquire Walter's Work Monocle, then scale Electromancer lightning bonuses or shift toward **Guardian 10 / Magic Engineering 10** if enemy chip damage threatens your health pool.

## ⚠️ Patch caution: Brass Mirror Fragment

Patch 1.0.23 rebalanced **Brass Mirror Fragment** to resolve an issue where sub-1 MP interactions triggered endless activations. The artifact remains mandatory for this build's mana economy, but always verify current MP recovery behavior after major game updates before committing a run.
