Minigun is the machine-gun ranged line — a firehose of crossbow bolts that trades burst for sheer volume. It's the most "documented" build on this list, with a video-verified clear behind the main variant.

## Why it works
> *Patch check 2026-08-27 — reviewed against 1.0.30: nothing in 1.0.26–1.0.30 touches this page's weapons, artifacts or mechanics. Recipe unchanged.*
The three core artifacts turn a fast crossbow into a sustained-DPS engine: **Blue Wrist Guard** feeds the rate, **Brass Mirror Fragment** keeps resources flowing, and **Walter's Work Monocle** sharpens each shot. Electromancer Miracle scales the lightning-flavored payload.

## The documented variant (video)

The **White Rabbit Hard 35** preset is the one run of this list you can watch before you try it — a full build-and-clear demo on video. Copy the preset code, then adjust artifacts to what your run offers.

## Tank variant (Scholar Lizard)

A community recipe for players who want the same gun with a wall in front: **Guardian 10 + Magic Engineering 10**. Slower kills, dramatically more forgiving — the recommended entry point if you keep dying on the pure-DPS version.

## Leveling order

1. The three-core-artifact triangle first — the engine doesn't work without all three.
2. Then lean either **Guardian 10 / Magic Engineering 10** (tank) or raw rate/crit (glass gun).

## ⚠️ Patch caution: Brass Mirror Fragment

1.0.23 treated **Brass Mirror's below-1 MP interaction as a bug** and rebalanced it. The artifact is still core to the recipe, but **re-verify the interaction after every patch** before trusting a fresh run to it.

## Evidence & patch notes

Main variant: **Hard 35 on video** (White Rabbit preset). Tank variant: community recipe. See the [six-build overview](/builds/best-builds) for the full comparison.
