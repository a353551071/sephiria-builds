The dagger is Sephiria's fastest, most actions-per-minute weapon, and it owns the single biggest quality-of-life change of the recent patches: as of **1.0.26**, Dash Attack can chain straight into Special Attack — Parry or Fury. That turns the family's signature dash-weave from "repositioning" into an actual damage-and-defense layer.

## What the 1.0.26 dash change actually does

Officially (Steam patch notes, 1.0.26): *"The Dagger's Dash Attack can now be chained into Special Attack: Parry and Special Attack: Fury."*

In practice, three consequences:

- **Dash is now an opener, not an escape.** You can dash through a pack and come out of it with a Parry already buffering — melee risk drops noticeably.
- **Fury routes get faster charge loops** whenever your route keeps Fury enabled (several Tier 2 evolutions disable it — see the tree below).
- **Routes built around Dash ATK itself** — Beyond the Threshold → Shadow of Hope — get the most indirect value, since you're dashing constantly anyway.

The freeze-control core of the family's most-proven build is untouched by this change; see the [Frozen Smelt build page](/builds/frozen-smelt) for that patch-check note.

## Which route should you play?

- **Freeze control (proven):** Sleeping Frost → **Frozen Smelt** is the family's verified endgame — ≈10K late DPS community-reported (≈17K with Berut's Sickle), Hard 60 claim on 1.0.24. Full recipe on the [Frozen Smelt build page](/builds/frozen-smelt).
- **Dash tempo (most affected by 1.0.26):** Beyond the Threshold → **Shadow of Hope** converts constant dashing into repeated Blade Prison hits — the 1.0.26 chain change gives this route free Parry safety between dashes. Community testing is still thin; treat it as the fresh-tech pick.
- **Elemental on-hit:** Lightning Dagger or Fire Dagger keep the classic fast-swing loop and add an element — Lightning's Wrath's stacking Electrocution suits long fights, Heated Blade's Dagger Throw turns the weapon into a ranged burner (at the cost of Parry).
- **Caster hybrid:** the Academy lines (Tome of Mimicry / Liberation) pair dagger speed with Grimoire builds — niche, but Liberation scaling off your *highest* damage type rewards stacked element builds.

For how the family stacks up against the other five, see the [weapon tier list](/guides/weapon-tier-list) — the dagger sits in A-tier as the control-first pick.
