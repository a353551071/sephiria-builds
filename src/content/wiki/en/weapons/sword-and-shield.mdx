> *Patch check 2026-08-28 — reviewed against 1.0.30: nothing in 1.0.26–1.0.30 touches the Sword & Shield family. Evolution recipes and effects below are current.*

Sword & Shield is Sephiria's defensive anchor: every member guards as well as swings, and half the family's Tier 3 upgrades convert *blocking itself* into damage. It's the pick the community most consistently recommends for a first clear — the weapon that lets you make mistakes and still finish the floor.

## Why the shield carries runs

Two things make this family the beginner standard:

- **Block economy.** Guard stops frontal damage while Cleave answers groups, and the family's upgrades lean into it: Perfect Guard can grant +20% All DMG, successful blocks can reflect 300–400% of incoming damage, and Shield Bash scales directly off DEF. A block-first run still deals damage — the block *is* the damage.
- **Four exclusive artifacts.** No other weapon family has more artifact support locked to it: **Radiating Charm** (block grants Physical DMG), **Riposte Blade Fragment** (block performs a counter up to 550% Physical DMG), **Artificial Fairy, Iphiel** (blocking regenerates MP), and the Legendary **Shieldmate** (up to −100% Special ATK MP cost plus Weapon DMG). If one appears in a shop, it's build-defining.

## Which route should you play?

- **Safe lane (the beginner pick):** **Unity of Offense and Defense → Indomitable** — Normal ATK guards 90° in front, then +33% Normal ATK DMG trades away Cleave. Simple, forgiving, still respectable damage.
- **Block economy:** **Protection of the Fuzz → Frozen Fury / Starlight / Stone Sword** — the whole trio converts blocking into offense: +20% All DMG windows off Perfect Guard, 300–400% reflect, or a DEF-scaling Shield Bash. Pair with the exclusive artifacts above.
- **Special-attack engine:** **Tiny Destruction → Grip of Fear** — stack Rising Dread, then dump it into one +200% range Special ATK. The family's ceiling route.
- **Elemental conversion:** **Magic Wand → Wings of Lightning / Breath of Ice / Gaze of Flames** — the missile trio converts to your element of choice, like the staff's elemental lines.
- **Artifact conductor:** **Frost Shard / Conductor / Incendium** — Tier 2s that hand the run to Frost Relic, Storm Cloud or Solar Blade artifacts; high ceiling, high artifact dependence.

For how the family ranks against the other five weapons, see the [weapon tier list](/guides/weapon-tier-list) — Sword & Shield sits in A-tier as the beginner anchor, and its cousin the [greatsword](/weapons/greatsword) is the S-tier special-attack pick.
