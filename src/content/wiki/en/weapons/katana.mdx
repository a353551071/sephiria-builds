The katana is Sephiria's most active melee weapon, and its 1.0 endgame form — **Biryuu** — has quietly become one of the community's favorite crit machines. If you like being in the enemy's face and watching numbers fly, this is your line.

## Evolution path
> *Patch check 2026-08-27 — reviewed against 1.0.30: nothing in 1.0.26–1.0.30 touches this page's weapons, artifacts or mechanics. Recipe unchanged.*
```
Katana → Cheongwangsak → Biryuu
```

> **Naming & sourcing note:** this path and Biryuu's double-classification mechanic are community-documented (r/sephiria and Steam discussions). [Kean's Sephiria Compendium](https://docs.google.com/spreadsheets/d/1uFp8Z8ANdgCSt_7EHbjGyQ9Rob9Z8EJOnom8elWcVMg/edit) lists the katana/blade family under different English display names — Famed Sword 'Muramasa', Rendblade, Thunder Sword 'Bronte' and friends — and doesn't use the name "Biryuu" or the normal/final-hit double classification. Treat both as the same family under different naming conventions.

The key upgrade happens at the final step: **Biryuu's attacks are counted as both normal attacks and final hits**. Most weapons choose one classification; Biryuu gets both. That means any effect that reads "on normal attack" *and* any effect that reads "on final hit" both trigger on the same swing — Firmness and Precision-style effects effectively stack twice.

## Core setup

- **Miracle:** Hunter
- **Costume:** Red Fox
- **Stat targets:** ~60% crit chance, ~100% crit damage
- **Priority:** attack speed and on-hit effects over raw attack damage — every extra swing is another double-dip

## Playing the build

Biryuu rewards aggressive rhythm: wide normal attacks scale your crit window, and final hits keep the on-hit economy running. In Hard mode, respect burst windows — the Red Fox costume's profile suits sustained aggression, but freeze and control supports (a Glacier dip, or companions that body-block) smooth out the dangerous floors.

## Tank katana variant (Tier 60)

If crit piloting is too spicy, [r/sephiria](https://www.reddit.com/r/sephiria/) has cleared Tier 60 with a **Blade Katana + maxed Guardian stat** route — sheathed attacks become very strong after the second upgrade, and defense scaling carries the run. Slower, dramatically more forgiving, and a great first Hard-mode clear. A related reddit tip: special attack priority matters more than weapon choice in the mid-game — grab special-attack artifacts when they appear.

## Who should pick katana?

Pick katana if you want an **active, high-actions-per-minute playstyle** with the game's widest crit scaling. Pick greatsword instead if you want a calmer, defense-first run (see the [weapon tier list](/guides/weapon-tier-list) for that comparison). And if you'd rather freeze everything before it swings back, the [dagger](/weapons/dagger)'s Frozen Smelt route is the control player's answer.
