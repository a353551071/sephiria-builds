> *Patch check 2026-08-27 — reviewed against 1.0.30: nothing in 1.0.26–1.0.30 touches the staff family. Evolution recipes and effects below are current.*

The staff is Sephiria's caster family — and its most branched. Where the melee weapons pick one identity, the staff splits into elements, spears and hybrids, so the "right" staff depends entirely on what you want to cast. Two routes are proven in the 1.0 meta, both covered on this page.

## Why the staff is the caster weapon

The family's core trick is **damage-type conversion**: several branches turn Weapon ATK damage into a single element, so a whole run budget can stack one element instead of spreading. The three elemental staffs are the cleanest example — Staff of Embers (Fire), Staff of Rime (Cold), Staff of Storms (Lightning) each give up raw Weapon ATK DMG to scale off their element.

Two routes are the proven endgame picks:

- **Ilonica — the homing caster.** Flourish Staff → **Ilonica's Spirit Staff** turns Normal ATK into a **Bolt Barrage** that casts random Bolt spells for free, and the bolts are homing. It's the most hands-off damage in the game — the [Ilonica Bolts build](/builds/ilonic-bolts) runs it with a Floor 50 claim, spreading Ember / Glacier / Magitech 4–6 each.
- **Formless Dance — the evasion melee-caster.** Javelin → **Formless Dance** makes Normal ATK homing with a chance to fire twice, scaling that chance per Evasion point. The [Formless Dance build](/builds/formless-dance) is the glass-ninja route (Duelist Miracle, Bloodstone Earrings, ~76% observed dodge cap).

## Which route should you play?

- **Homing caster (proven A-tier):** **Flourish Staff → Ilonica's Spirit Staff** — three-element homing bolts, the most hands-off raw output in the game. Needs the Elementalist Miracle and a balanced 4–6 spread in Ember / Glacier / Magitech; the [Ilonica Bolts build](/builds/ilonic-bolts) has the full recipe.
- **Evasion melee-caster (B-tier, hard):** **Javelin → Formless Dance** — close-range homing attacks with a dodge stat doing the tanking. Watch the ~76% observed dodge cap; the [Formless Dance build](/builds/formless-dance) warns against over-investing.
- **Elemental conversion:** Staff of Embers → Fiaola's / Solis Braca (fire), Staff of Rime → Malaspina (cold), Staff of Storms → Viduka / Nephorhax (lightning) — pick the element your run is already stacking.
- **Spear line:** Georoid Spear → Ruadhslea / Fuarsca — a melee-caster that moves through Dash Points.
- **Block-and-empower:** Divine Iron Staff → Weight of the Sea / Scale of the Astral Sea — blocking feeds the next swing; pairs with block-heavy Miracle setups.

For how the family ranks against the other five weapons, see the [weapon tier list](/guides/weapon-tier-list) — the staff sits in A-tier behind the katana and greatsword.
