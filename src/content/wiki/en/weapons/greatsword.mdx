The greatsword is Sephiria's heaviest-hitting melee family — and its identity is the **special attack**. While the katana wins through crit double-dips and the dagger through freeze control, greatsword routes can turn the Special button into the whole build. It's one of the two S-tier families in the 1.0 meta, with the most forgiving beginner route in the game.

## Why the greatsword is the special-attack weapon

Two pieces make the special-attack build work, and both are on this page:

- **The Hypersensitivity line.** Hypersensitivity gives +20% Special ATK DMG and 50% faster Special ATK charging. Its Tier 3s stack the special attack to absurd heights: **Loving Gaze** (+100% Special ATK crit chance), **Negligence** (special attacks charge *instantly*), and **Black Iron Halberd** (bigger range, +50% DMG on the other edge's hit).
- **The Absolute Ring artifact.** It grants up to +30% Special ATK Range and evolves after killing 40 enemies with the Special Attack — the named piece behind the tier list's "special button is the whole build" summary. The community's most-liked greatsword build (Creaking Spine) pairs it with Amethyst and Rock Elephant.

## Which route should you play?

- **Special-attack monster (proven S-tier):** Hypersensitivity → **Loving Gaze** or **Negligence**, stacked with the **Absolute Ring** artifact — this is the route the tier list calls out as the family's ceiling. High skill ceiling, but the numbers are absurd.
- **Beginner defense (most forgiving):** **Bone Gearblade → Creaking Spine** — Reassemble turns your swings into slow, safe, scaling hits; the [Creaking Spine build page](/builds/creaking-spine) runs it with Will/Patience 20 and the Valet Miracle. The lowest legendary-weapon dependence of any endgame route.
- **Elemental on-hit:** Frostbound Greatsword / The Emberforged / Lightning "E2G" keep the classic heavy-swing loop and add an element — simplest to pilot, each with a clear Tier 3 capstone (Eternal Frost, Solis Cineris, E3G's Storm Cloud engine).
- **Wound/Chaos nuker:** Crimson Thorn → **The Inquisitor** converts the family into a debuff detonator — niche, but the only route that turns the greatsword's weight into a screen-wide Chaos burst.

## Sword & Shield: the sister family

Sword & Shield shares the greatsword's base and is the pick widely recommended as the **strongest true beginner weapon**. Three routes to know:

- **Unity of Offense and Defense → Indomitable** — the block-first line: Normal ATK guards 90° in front, then +33% Normal ATK DMG (Cleave off). The safe lane.
- **Protection of the Fuzz → Frozen Fury** — block econ into damage: successful blocks cut MP costs, and Perfect Guard gives +20% All DMG for 14s.
- **Tiny Destruction → Grip of Fear** — the offense-first shield: +40% Special ATK DMG scaling into a Rising Dread stack dump (+30% Special ATK DMG per stack).

For how the whole family ranks against the other five weapons, see the [weapon tier list](/guides/weapon-tier-list) — the greatsword sits in S-tier as the special-attack pick.
