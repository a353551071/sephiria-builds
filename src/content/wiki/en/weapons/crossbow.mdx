> *Patch check 2026-08-27 — reviewed against 1.0.30: nothing in 1.0.26–1.0.30 touches the crossbow family. Evolution recipes and effects below are current.*

The crossbow is Sephiria's only ammo-based weapon: every member runs on **Ammo / Magazine / Reload** instead of the endless swing the melee families use. That makes uptime the whole game — fire-rate and reload-recovery matter as much as raw damage, and several evolutions turn the ammo itself into the build.

## Why the crossbow is the machine-gun weapon

Two pieces make the machine-gun build work, and both live on the Lightning branch:

- **Unstable Lightning Mark.** Normal ATKs get a **40% chance to fire a Lightning Arrow (160% Lightning DMG)**, which also zaps a random nearby enemy for another 160%. It's the family's engine — everything on the branch is built on that arrow.
- **Minigun.** The branch capstone: **+10% Lightning Arrow chance**, and Special becomes **Barrage** — a volley of bullets at 1 MP per shot that can itself proc Lightning Arrows. Fifteen rounds of machine-gun lightning, sustained.

The core artifact set behind this route is **Blue Wrist Guard** (rate), **Brass Mirror Fragment** (resource loop) and **Walter's Work Monocle** (shot sharpening), run with the **Electromancer Miracle** — see the [Minigun build page](/builds/minigun) for the full recipe, including a video-verified Hard 35 clear.

## Which route should you play?

- **Machine-gun Lightning (proven A-tier):** **Unstable Lightning Mark → Minigun** — the family's ceiling and the most-documented build on the site. Siblings **Mark Stabilization** (Electrocution on Lightning Arrows) and **Cloud Gatherer** (Storm Clouds off them) are the alternatives off the same mark.
- **Reload-econ dash loop:** **Reload Device → Bounty Hunter** — dash to reload instantly, +7% DMG per dash count. The most mobile crossbow; aggressive, gap-closing play.
- **Cold scaling:** **Frost Veil → Expansion Device** — the buff converts Normal ATKs to Cold and the capstone pierces with Frostbite. Cozy, safe, scales with your cold stats.
- **Fire:** **Fire Mine → Mini Cannon** — grenade lobbing at range; scales off Fire DMG.
- **Poison:** **Kagumede → Purple Jacket** — the short-reload spam line, stacking Poison.
- **Piercing:** **M-9199 → XRA-9** — bullets punch through crowds; Special compresses a whole magazine into one shot.

For how the family ranks against the other five weapons, see the [weapon tier list](/guides/weapon-tier-list) — the crossbow sits in A-tier behind the katana and greatsword.
