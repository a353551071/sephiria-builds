Sephiria's 1.0 balance is unusually healthy: community clears have been recorded on [every weapon family](https://steamcommunity.com/app/2436940/discussions/0/588434434320216892/), and the honest answer to "which weapon is best" is *the one whose playstyle you enjoy*. What we *can* rank is how strong each route's ceiling is, how much artifact support it has, and how hard it is to pilot. All rankings below reflect patch 1.0.2x clears and discussion on [r/sephiria](https://www.reddit.com/r/sephiria/).

## Tier Explanations

- **S tier** — Highest verified ceilings with deep artifact support. Multiple endgame builds.
- **A tier** — Proven Hard-mode clears, slightly narrower ceilings or tighter requirements.
- **B tier** — Viable and fun, but currently more niche or waiting on 1.0 re-verification.

## S Tier <TierBadge tier="S" />

### Katana — crit scaling that double-dips

The Katana line ends at **Biryuu**, whose attacks count as *both* normal attacks and final hits. That double classification lets Firmness and Precision-style effects stack on every swing — the widest crit scaling in the game. A tank variant (Blade Katana + maxed Guardian stat) has also cleared Tier 60. See our [Katana build page](/weapons/katana) for the full recipe.

### Greatsword — special attack monster

Greatsword routes revolve around special attacks: **Absolute Ring** and Amethyst-crystal scaling turn the special button into the whole build, with companion and mana damage supporting it. The **Bone Sword → Creaking Spine** line is frequently called the most beginner-friendly endgame route, and one-button variants exist on YouTube. Sword & Shield in the same family is widely recommended as the strongest true beginner pick. See the [Greatsword weapon page](/weapons/greatsword) for every evolution in the family.

## A Tier <TierBadge tier="A" />

### Dagger — freeze control

Dagger → Sleeping Frost → **Frozen Smelt** freezes the screen and stacks Glacier + Precision. Community reports put late-game DPS around 10K (≈17K with Berut's Sickle equipped), with a Hard 60 claim on 1.0.24. Control-first playstyle that trades raw ceiling for safety.

> **1.0.26 update:** Dash Attack can now chain into Special Attack (Parry/Fury). A straight buff to the family's melee layer — the freeze-control ceiling above still stands; family rating under re-verification. See the [Frozen Smelt build page](/builds/frozen-smelt) for the patch-check note, and the [Dagger weapon page](/weapons/dagger) for the full family breakdown and what the dash chain changes in practice.

### Heavy Crossbow — the Minigun

Heavy Crossbow → Unstable Lightning Mark → **Minigun** is the community's favorite machine-gun route: Blue Wrist Guard, Brass Mirror Fragment and Walter's Work Monocle form the core, with a recorded Hard 35 clear and multiple preset codes shared. Watch patch notes — the 1.0.23 Brass Mirror interaction was treated as a bug. See the [Crossbow weapon page](/weapons/crossbow) for every evolution in the family.

### Staff — caster and hybrid casters

The most branched family: Throwing Spear → **Formless Dance** (evasion melee-caster) and Flame Staff → **Elemental Fusion Staff Ilonica** (homing three-element bolts, Floor 50 claim). Flexible, but each variant wants specific stat spreads (crit for Dance, 4–6 in each element for Ilonica). See the [Staff weapon page](/weapons/staff) for every evolution in the family.

## B Tier <TierBadge tier="B" />

### Scythe — execute specialist

Scythe-of-Berut crit builds ("Crit God") show up in YouTube Hard-mode content and pair naturally with other crit routes as a swap weapon. Strong flavor, fewer documented full-run recipes on 1.0 than the families above.

## Patch watch: 1.0.26 – 1.0.30

Last full review: **2026-08-27, against 1.0.30** (Steam patch notes).

- **1.0.26** — Dagger: Dash Attack now chains into Special Attack (Parry/Fury) — see the A-tier note above. Multiplayer temporary-HP rules changed; Chapter 6 final-boss fix.
- **1.0.27 / 1.0.28 / 1.0.29** — bug-fix only (UI, multiplayer, saves). No tier impact.
- **1.0.30** — **Frozen Bow: arrows now pierce walls** (community-requested). The Bow family isn't ranked on this page yet — that change puts it on the re-verification shortlist. Flourish Staff charge QoL. Official Traditional Chinese / Italian / Turkish added.

Nothing else in 1.0.26–1.0.30 touches the ranked weapons above.

## How this list was made

Rankings weigh: (1) verified Hard-mode clears on 1.0.2x, (2) artifact and Miracle support depth, (3) how forgiving the route is for players still learning the game. When a new patch lands, re-verify before investing heavily — [r/sephiria](https://www.reddit.com/r/sephiria/) and the Steam discussions are where fresh clears surface first.
