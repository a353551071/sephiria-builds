Sephiria is a fate-defying action roguelite by Team Horay (the studio behind Dungreed): a bunny climbing a demon-filled tower, collecting weapons, artifacts and permanent upgrades between runs. The systems stack deeper than they first look — this guide gets you through the first hours without wasting your early resources.

## Pick a forgiving weapon first
> *Patch check 2026-08-27 — reviewed against 1.0.30: nothing in 1.0.26–1.0.30 touches this page's weapons, artifacts or mechanics. Recipe unchanged.*
Your first runs should teach you the game, not punish you. Two community-consensus picks:

- **Sword & Shield / Greatsword family** — widely called the strongest beginner route. Special attacks carry damage, shields carry mistakes. The endgame **Creaking Spine** line has the lowest legendary dependence of any build.
- **Tank Katana** (Blade Katana + Guardian stat) — if you prefer faster attacks with defense scaling.

Save the glass-cannon routes (Biryuu crit, Ilonica caster) for your second or third weapon mastery. Full rankings live in the [weapon tier list](/guides/weapon-tier-list).

## Understand the four layers

Every Sephiria build is four layers stacking on the same skeleton:

1. **Weapon evolution** — each weapon line evolves twice (e.g. Katana → Cheongwangsak → Biryuu). The final form defines what your run wants.
2. **Stats** — spread across attack stats (crit chance/damage, elemental schools), defense stats (Guardian, Will, Patience) and utility (Wisdom for MP). Builds target thresholds, not even spreads — e.g. ~60% crit / ~100% crit damage for crit routes.
3. **Miracles & costumes** — your Miracle (Hunter, Duelist, Valet, Electromancer, Elementalist…) and costume set the run's identity. Pick to match the weapon's win condition.
4. **Artifacts** — run pickups that do the compounding. Start with artifacts that boost what you already do (special attack, on-hit, freeze); a build you can pilot beats a stronger build you can't.

## First three runs: a checklist

- Clear the early tower floors with special-attack priority — reddit's universal tip is that **special attacks matter more than weapon choice** early.
- Grab Absolute Ring-class special-attack artifacts when offered; they carry mid-game damage on almost every route.
- Spend meta-progression on **survivability first** (HP, defense), damage second. The tower punishes greed early and rewards it late.
- Bank one "rescue" plan: if a run stalls, pivoting to a documented preset ([r/sephiria](https://www.reddit.com/r/sephiria/) and the [Steam build thread](https://steamcommunity.com/app/2436940/discussions/0/588434434320216892/) share codes) beats forcing a dead route.

## When to jump to Hard mode

Clear the tower once on Normal with any weapon you understand, then take a verified 1.0 recipe from our [best builds page](/builds/best-builds) into Hard. Hard mode is where Sephiria's build depth actually starts — and where every claim in our guides got verified.
