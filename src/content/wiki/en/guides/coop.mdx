Sephiria is a much better story with friends: online co-op for up to **4 players**, all climbing the same tower, all rearranging the same kind of backpack, and all blaming the lag when a boss pattern wins. Here's how the party systems actually work — including the rough edges nobody puts on the store page.

## Party basics

- **Up to 4 players online.** One host runs the tower; everyone shares the floors, the shops and the chaos.
- **You can drop items for each other.** Drop an item off the side of your inventory and it falls to the ground for teammates to grab. Ember artifact dropped for your burn partner; grimoire passed to your bolts player — trading picks is a real part of co-op drafting.
- **Reviving a downed teammate costs 50% of your *current* health** — not max health. It's much more forgiving than it sounds in other games: you revive with whatever you have left, so a low-HP rescue still works, it just leaves you fragile.

## ⚠️ The ping warning: dodges desync past ~100ms

This is the one thing to know before you queue with someone overseas. **Once ping goes past ~100ms, dodge timing desyncs** — you have to dodge *before* the attack lands rather than when it would hit. In a game this strict about dash i-frames, that's not a minor annoyance; it changes how the combat feels at a fundamental level.

Practical takeaway:

- **Same region → co-op is great.** Same continent is where Sephiria co-op shines.
- **Cross-ocean partners → expect to pre-dodge**, or treat the session as single-player with a spectator.
- If runs keep falling apart on timing, test whether it's ping before anyone rerolls their build.

## ⚠️ Controller support: full on paper, spotty in practice

Controller support is listed as full, but player reports include **buttons not registering, UI flashing, and dash inputs sticking** so your character keeps running — on some hardware. If you're controller-only, don't buy blind for the co-op: confirm your specific pad works (the demo on the store page is a free test) before committing to a co-op campaign.

## Builds that co-op well together

The chaos is the fun — and some pairs compound it:

- **Meteor + bolts**: a burn/meteor player blankets the screen in fire while a grimoire-bolts player cleans everything up (see the [meteor build](/builds/meteor)). Area denial plus on-hit execution is the classic co-op comp.
- **Support-flavored costumes**: the Wing-Eared Rabbit shares self-buffs with teammates within 5 tiles and revives downed players 30% faster — the closest thing Sephiria has to a dedicated party role (full trade-offs on the [characters page](/guides/characters)).
- **Coordinate artifact drafts**: since you can drop items, agree early on who's collecting what — two players fighting over the same ember artifacts is half a build each.

## Progression note: the true final boss

Story progress is **shared through the host's run**. Clearing **chapter 6** is what unlocks the true final encounter — if your group is heading for the ending, plan the campaign around getting the host (or everyone) through chapter 6 rather than re-clearing early floors.
