In Sephiria you play a rabbit climbing a demon tower, but "the rabbit" is 27 different costumes. The in-game **Costumes** tab is the character select: every entry trades one stat for another and most are gated behind an unlock. This page is the full 1.0.30 roster — the trade-off and unlock for each costume, plus which of our builds each one fits.

> **A note on "characters":** some Sephiria wiki sites list "characters" — those are the **NPCs** (Elru and the rest). The *playable* roster lives in the Costumes tab, which most guides never document. This page covers the playable roster only. Gameplay data comes from [Kean's Sephiria Compendium](https://docs.google.com/spreadsheets/d/1uFp8Z8ANdgCSt_7EHbjGyQ9Rob9Z8EJOnom8elWcVMg/edit), Costumes tab.

## Which costume fits which build

The costume is a build slot, not a skin. Four pairings are documented in our build guides; the rest are read straight off the stat trade-offs above.

**Documented in our build guides:**

- **Red Fox** → [Biryuu Katana crit](/builds/biryuu-katana). +6 inventory slots is the reason the crit build runs Red Fox; the −50% healing is manageable when the whole build is dodging instead of tanking.
- **Ghost** → [Ilonica caster](/builds/ilonic-bolts). Grimoires at 0 MP is the whole elemental-spread engine; you trade 40% weapon damage you weren't using anyway.
- **White Rabbit** → [Minigun](/builds/minigun). The documented Hard 35 preset plays White Rabbit — +12% Special ATK feeds the machine-gun's special spam.
- **Scholar Lizard** → [Minigun tank variant](/builds/minigun). 20 Max HP is brutal, but Mana Reflow removes MP limits entirely — a glass-cannon trade that only works if you never get hit.

**Suggested by the stat trade-offs (not yet documented in a build guide):**

- **Armored Bunny** → [Sword & Shield](/weapons/sword-and-shield). The family converts defense into offense via a DEF-scaling Shield Bash, so +10 DEF feeds damage as well as survivability.
- **Otter** → crit routes like [Biryuu Katana](/builds/biryuu-katana) as a Red Fox alternative. +9% crit with only −8% move speed, and no healing curse.
- **Skeleton** → any burst route. +20% all damage with a Curse Tablet start and no listed trade-off — worth the 333-damage unlock quest.
- **Deer** → never take it on a crit build (−90% Critical Chance). It exists for Wishing Fountain setups; its +3 fountain capacity is its whole identity.
- **Wing-Eared / Wizard Bunny / Frog / Red-Clothed Cat** → the four caster opens. Each starts a run with a spell (Blessing / Ice Bolt / Lightning Bolt / Fire Bolt) at 0.8× physical damage — free casting identity before you find a grimoire.
- **Adventurer Bunny** → dash-focused melee ([dagger](/weapons/dagger) dash-cancel routes). +1 dash point and +10% recovery, at the cost of grimoire haste you don't need on a dash build.

### Safest starters

Pink Bunny and Forest Cat have no downside at all. Armored Bunny is the forgiving tank, Adventurer Bunny the balanced dash option. Save Red Fox, Scholar Lizard and Skeleton for later — their trade-offs (healing, 20 HP, self-damage) punish a player still learning the tower.

## How this was verified

- **Roster data**: [Kean's Sephiria Compendium](https://docs.google.com/spreadsheets/d/1uFp8Z8ANdgCSt_7EHbjGyQ9Rob9Z8EJOnom8elWcVMg/edit), Costumes tab — a community-maintained live document, read 2026-08-28. Values drift with patches; re-check before you invest a run in an unlock.
- **Portraits**: official character FaceChips from the Team Horay press kit. 7 of 9 map to roster entries (5 exact asset-name matches, plus RedHoly → Red-Hooded Rabbit and Mage → Wizard Bunny confirmed visually). The remaining 2 (Holy, Rabbit13) are shown with what's visibly in the image only — we don't guess an identity we can't confirm.
- **Costume cards** (gallery, below the FaceChips): in-game costume cards captured from the Steam Community guide ["Basic Guide to Sephiria"](https://steamcommunity.com/sharedfiles/filedetails/?id=3474238982) by FireBrother — the best visual reference for most roster entries, since the game's own store screenshots don't show them. **They're from an older build (0.7.x): treat them as illustrations, not stat evidence.** Some card values no longer match 1.0.30 — e.g. the Brown Rabbit card shows +6 Evasion, while the current roster gives +12. The table above stays on the compendium's current values.
- **Patch check 2026-08-28** — reviewed against 1.0.30: nothing in 1.0.26–1.0.30 changed the costume roster or its unlocks. The build pairings above were verified against their dedicated guide pages, which carry their own patch checks.
